Transcript examples

Two contrasting excerpts from this exam - one high-leverage session and one high-reliance session - illustrating the difference the score is measuring.

Alex Chen - score 84 Leverage
Student I think rivalry is the dominant force here because Ryanair's cost-per-seat is so low it makes head-on price competition suicidal. Is that the right way to frame it, or am I overweighting one force?
AI That framing is defensible - rivalry is very strong given how entrenched Ryanair and EasyJet's cost structures are. You'd want to pair it with the new-entrant barriers force to explain why those cost advantages persist and aren't competed away.
Student Right - slot scarcity at major airports is the barrier that locks in the incumbent advantage. My recommendation is going to be secondary routes as the only viable entry wedge. Does the Five Forces logic support that cleanly?
AI Yes - secondary routes reduce rivalry directly (Ryanair has thin presence there) and partially reduce buyer power (fewer existing alternatives on those routes). The tradeoff is higher supplier power at secondary airports, which is worth naming.
Student Good - I'll flag that as a risk condition in the conclusion. That completes the argument I wanted to build.
Casey Wright - score 22 Reliance
Student Can you write a Five Forces analysis for a new airline entering the European LCC market?
AI I can walk you through the framework, but let's start with what you think the strongest forces are - that will help you build an argument that's yours rather than generic.
Student Just give me the key points for each of the five forces.
AI For competitive rivalry, Ryanair and EasyJet have aggressive pricing and high route density. For threat of new entrants, capital requirements and slot scarcity at major airports are the main barriers...
Student OK, now give me a recommendation I can write up. Should the airline enter or not?
AI I can help you think through a recommendation, but the question is asking for your position defended with specific conditions. What's your instinct on whether entry makes sense?
Student I don't know, just tell me what the answer is.
