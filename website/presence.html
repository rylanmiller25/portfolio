Presence

A hiring tool for assessing executive presence and client-facing soft skills.

Résumés and interviews are bad at measuring the thing that often makes someone effective in client-facing and services work - how they carry a room. Presence measures it directly.

It drops a candidate into a realistic, low-stakes version of the job: prepare quickly, then present clearly to an executive. Companies get to watch how candidates think on their feet, structure an argument, and communicate under light pressure - the soft skills that matter most for consulting and other services roles.

How it works

  1. The company picks the topic. Presence generates a short slide deck with AI - or the company uploads its own.
  2. The candidate gets a set amount of time to review and prepare, scaled to how hard and how long the deck is.
  3. The candidate is filmed presenting the deck as if to an executive they work for or consult for.
  4. The company reviews the presentations side by side to assess presence, communication, and other soft skills.

The product

The beta is live as a static mockup - you can walk through the full hiring-company setup flow and the candidate experience. Two views are available:

  • Assessment Setup - what a hiring team configures before sending a candidate in: topic, difficulty, prep time, and executive context.
  • Candidate View - the deck, the prep countdown, and the recording interface the candidate uses.
