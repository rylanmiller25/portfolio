Presence Beta

A working look at the platform - what hiring companies configure, and what candidates actually see.

The beta has two views. The client setup view shows what a hiring team configures before dispatching a candidate to an assessment - topic, difficulty, prep time, and the executive context the candidate presents to. The candidate view shows the slide deck itself, the prep countdown, and the recording interface.

This is a static mockup with realistic sample content. The example assessment is a consulting-style market entry case, pre-filled as a firm might configure it for a Business Analyst candidate.

Beta views

Client view Assessment Setup

Configure a new assessment - choose the slide topic, number of slides, difficulty, prep-time limit, and the executive role the candidate will present to.

Candidate view Deck + Recording

The candidate's screen - a prep countdown, the example slide deck to review, and the interface for recording their presentation to a stand-in executive.
